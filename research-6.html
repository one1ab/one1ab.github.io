Integrated Circuits

Scaling from single hero devices to reliable, wafer-scale 2D integrated circuits.

Why It Matters

A single great transistor does not make a computer. Real-world impact requires thousands to millions of uniform devices working together as inverters, logic gates, SRAM cells, and neural arrays with high yield, tight device-to-device uniformity, and clean signal routing.

ONE 1ab Research Focus

  • 2D complementary logic circuits (inverters, NAND/NOR, multiplexers, SRAM)
  • Device-to-device uniformity, noise margin optimization, and SPICE compact modeling
  • Monolithic 3D integration combining logic, memory, and sensing layers

How It Works

  1. Complementary CMOS Logic: Integrating matched n-type and p-type 2D transistors yields full rail-to-rail inverters, NAND/NOR gates, and ring oscillators with minimal static power.
  2. Uniformity & Yield Engineering: Wafer-scale synthesis, standardized lithography, and compact modeling ensure predictable circuit operation across the entire die.
  3. Monolithic 3D (M3D) Stacking: Low-temperature 2D fabrication lets logic, memory, and sensor tiers stack vertically with ultra-short interconnects.

Real-World Applications

3D Stacked SoCs

Ultra-dense chips uniting computation, memory, and sensors vertically

Ultra-Thin Edge Controllers

Compact integrated circuits for IoT and smart tags

Hardware AI Accelerators

Integrated synaptic arrays paired with 2D peripheral logic

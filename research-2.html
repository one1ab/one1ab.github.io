Synthesis of 2D Materials

Growing wafer-scale, defect-controlled 2D crystals atom by atom.

Why It Matters

Discovering great properties in a tiny mechanically exfoliated flake is only the first step. To turn 2D materials into real chips and sensors, we must synthesize large-area, uniform, single-crystalline films with precise control over layer number, doping, and defect density.

ONE 1ab Research Focus

  • Wafer-scale growth of monolayer and few-layer MoS₂, WS₂, WSe₂, and alloyed TMDs
  • Controlled substitutional doping and defect engineering for tailored conductivity
  • Low-temperature synthesis compatible with flexible substrates and silicon BEOL

How It Works

  1. Chemical Vapor Deposition (CVD / MOCVD): Gaseous metal and chalcogen precursors react at controlled temperatures and pressures to nucleate atomically thin layers.
  2. Layer & Grain Control: Tuning gas flow, substrate surface chemistry, and growth kinetics steers nucleation from isolated triangles into continuous wafer-scale monolayers.
  3. Clean Transfer & Hetero-Growth: Direct low-temperature synthesis and polymer-free transfer preserve pristine electronic quality for device fabrication.

Real-World Applications

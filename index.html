Overwhelming Nano Electronics Laboratory

Welcome to overwhelming Nano Electronics (ONE) Laboratory's homepage.

We are conducting basic and applied research at the frontier of nanomaterials, artificial intelligence, neuromorphic engineering,memory device and applications, flexible nanoelectronics, photonics, integrated circuits, and sensors.

Our passion lies in the discovery, understanding, and application of new paradigms to enable novel ubiquitous systems that can address societal needs.

We are looking for bright, motivated students and scholars to join the research group. A strong work ethic and an abundance of passion is essential for our research activities. Please contact for more information.
